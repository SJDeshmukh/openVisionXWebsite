Frequently asked questions

Understanding TapInX

What is an AI attendance system?

An AI attendance system uses computer vision and identity workflows to assist with recording presence, organizing attendance events and reducing repetitive manual entry.

Which businesses can use TapInX?

TapInX has configurable workflows for factories and daily-wage teams, hostels and managed residences, and schools and campuses.

Are AttendX and RouteX required?

No. TapInX, AttendX and RouteX are independent OpenVisionX products. A business can deploy TapInX on its own.
